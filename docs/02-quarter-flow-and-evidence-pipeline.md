## 2. Cost taxonomy — three categories, three behaviours

Costs must be separated because they do not behave the same way.

### 2.1 Recurring costs (automatic)
Paid every quarter regardless of decisions.

- Salaries
- Rent
- Software
- Insurance
- Cloud
- Maintenance

### 2.2 Decision-based costs
Created by the student's choices.

- Marketing campaigns
- Hiring
- R&D
- Training
- New equipment
- PR campaigns

### 2.3 Variable operating costs
Generated only if the business performs activity.

- Manufacturing cost per unit
- Shipping
- Payment gateway fees
- Warranty claims
- Customer support cost
- Returns

### Resulting execution order for the backend

1. **Recurring Cost Engine** → unavoidable expenses
2. **Budget Allocation (Finance)** → planned investments
3. **Simulation Engine** → operational costs generated by the consequences of decisions

This mirrors how companies actually spend money and makes cash-flow and P&L reports realistic.

## 3. The core engine chain

```
Student Decision
        ↓
Company Variables Change
        ↓
Department KPIs Change
        ↓
Company Performance Changes
        ↓
Evidence Collected
        ↓
Cognitive Scores
```

**Critical design principle: you never score the decision directly. You score its evidence.**

### Worked example

Marketing receives ₹12L. Student spends:

- ₹8L Performance
- ₹2L SEO
- ₹2L Community

The engine does **not** say `Strategic Thinking = 9/10`. Instead it stores **evidence**:

- Marketing heavily focused on immediate demand
- Still invested in SEO
- Still invested in Community
- Balanced short-term and long-term investment

## 4. Cross-department effects become variables

```
Marketing generates:  Leads = 1,500
Sales reads:          Leads = 1,500
                      Sales Capacity = 900
Result:               600 Leads Lost

Sales then updates:   Revenue ↓
                      Customer Satisfaction ↓
                      Brand Trust ↓
```

**Marketing isn't blamed. Sales isn't blamed. The company changes.**

## 5. Evidence structure

Rather than emitting a trait score, emit observations:

| Evidence | Observation |
|---|---|
| Budget Diversification | Yes |
| Balanced Investment | Yes |
| Cash Reserved | Yes |
| Long-term Investment | Yes |
| Risk Taken | Medium |
| Ignored Operations | No |

The Cognitive Engine reads these. **Strategic Thinking is calculated from patterns, not from profit.**

### Example weighting

| Evidence | Weight |
|---|---|
| Balanced Budget | +3 |
| Diversified Marketing | +2 |
| Long-term Investment | +3 |
| Consistent Objective | +2 |
| **Strategic Thinking** | **= 10** |

### Adaptability example — scoring behaviour, not outcomes

| Sequence | Evidence | Adaptability |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 SEO fails → Q2 student changes strategy | Pivoted Quickly = YES | 5 |
| Q2 SEO fails → Q3 student repeats same mistake | Ignored Feedback = YES | 5 |

The guiding question: *"What evidence would convince an investor that this founder thinks strategically?"*

Observable behaviours:

- Preserved cash when runway was short
- Balanced immediate growth with long-term investment
- Adjusted strategy after new information
- Didn't overreact to one bad quarter
- Aligned department spending with company goals

## 10. The dual-pipeline thesis

Most business simulations have one pipeline: `Decision → Result`.

**Myelin has two parallel pipelines:**

```
Decision
   │
   ├────────► Business Pipeline
   │          (What happened to the company?)
   │
   └────────► Evidence Pipeline
              (What kind of CEO made this decision?)
```

- The **Business Pipeline** determines cash, profit, valuation, and reports.
- The **Evidence Pipeline** determines strategic thinking, adaptability, leadership, systems thinking, and the other cognitive scores.

Responsibility split:

| Engine | Answers |
|---|---|
| Simulation engine | "What happened to the company?" |
| Evidence engine | "What kind of CEO is this student?" |
| Cognitive scoring engine | "What do those patterns tell us about their leadership?" |

This separation is what makes Myelin fundamentally different from traditional business
simulations: it assesses company performance and leadership quality **independently**.
